03 — How I work

From mark to market.

01

Listen & position

Every identity starts as a conversation, not a moodboard. I dig for the thing the business actually believes, then find the shortest visual route to saying it.

  • Discovery
  • Positioning
  • Naming support
02

Build the identity

Marks, type systems, colour, layout rules, packaging, book covers. Then a guideline document so the brand survives everyone who touches it after me.

  • Visual identity
  • Brand guidelines
  • Packaging & print
03

Design the interface

Mobile-first, always. Thumb-reachable controls, honest typography, motion that explains rather than decorates — and states designed for offline, empty and error.

  • UI systems
  • Prototyping
  • Motion & sound
04

Ship it myself

I build and release what I design — landing pages, PWAs, Android builds. Eight products are live because the handoff never had to leave the room.

  • Web development
  • PWA & release
  • Maintenance

04 — About

Graphic designer,
brand manager,
reluctant developer.

By profession I'm a graphic designer and brand manager. In practice that means I spend my days making businesses look and sound like themselves — and my nights turning small daily annoyances into apps.

WeGather.Studio has been the home for that since 2018: brand identities, guideline systems, packaging, book covers, decks, and websites we design and build ourselves. The products on this page came out of the same studio muscle — a real problem, an identity, an interface, then a release.
